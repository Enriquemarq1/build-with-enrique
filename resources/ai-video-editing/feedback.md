# Give useful editing feedback

Use three short statements: **keep this, change this, here is why**.

Example:

- Keep the simple two-color look and the camera position.
- Begin with the cup on the desk and the person's hand away from their face.
- The animation needs a clear starting position before they reach for the cup.

Give a timestamp or reference frame when it matters. Explain the desired effect on the viewer, not only the effect you want added.

```text
Review this preview against the selected style.
Keep: [what works].
Change: [specific issue and timestamp].
Why: [what should become clearer or feel different].
Apply the correction and show the affected passage.
If this teaches a reusable rule, propose the change to the style guide.
Keep shot-specific details in this project's feedback file.
```

When a result is approved, ask the agent to show the actual file changes. Conversation alone does not prove the guidance was saved.
