# Style references

Add links or files you have permission to use. For each reference, record:

- Source/title and relevant timestamp or frame.
- What to learn: captions, pacing, composition, motion or sound.
- What not to carry over: another person's logo, identity or unrelated treatment.
- Whether you inspected a still image, watched motion or listened to audio.

A screenshot cannot establish how the music or animation behaves.
