# My editing style

Status: template — replace the prompts below with your choices.

- **Name and purpose:** What do you call the style, and what content suits it?
- **References:** Which files/examples matter, and what should each teach the agent?
- **Pacing:** Where should the edit feel quick, calm or deliberate?
- **Captions:** Coverage, font, grouping, emphasis, position and readability.
- **Visuals:** Color, composition, illustration/photo treatment and when B-roll helps.
- **Motion:** How objects, text and camera move; what should stay consistent.
- **Sound:** Selected sound vocabulary, music role and space for clear speech.
- **Assets:** Exact paths to fonts, sound effects and reusable files.
- **Keep consistent:** The decisions that define the style.
- **Allow variation:** What can change to fit the video's meaning or speaker?
- **Approved examples:** Link reviewed outputs and explain what they demonstrate.
- **Known limits:** What still needs testing or manual judgment?

Review the treatment on another clip before calling it reliably reusable. Keep one authoritative style guide; link to it rather than copying conflicting versions into multiple agent skills.
